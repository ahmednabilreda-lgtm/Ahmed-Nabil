01 — Overview

Directing geospatial systems behind national-scale urban decisions.

Ahmed is a GIS and geospatial systems expert with more than 20 years of experience spanning enterprise GIS implementation, spatial data infrastructure (SDI) and digital transformation across Saudi Arabia's urban development and housing sectors.
